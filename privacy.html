Money Bus Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Money Bus stores game progress, settings, daily-challenge activity, and leaderboard entries locally on your device. We do not require an account and do not receive this game data.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Subject to your region, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its partners may collect or process device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, diagnostics, approximate location derived from IP address, and other data needed to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize ads. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform where required and may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before tracking.

You can decline tracking when prompted and manage applicable consent choices through the consent form. Apple device-level tracking permission can also be changed in iOS Settings.

Third parties

Google's handling of advertising data is governed by its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Money Bus is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

Changes
