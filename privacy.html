Money Bus Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Money Bus stores game progress, settings, daily-challenge activity, and leaderboard entries locally on your device. We do not require an account and do not receive this game data.

Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display non-personalized advertisements. Google and its partners may collect or process device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction, diagnostics, approximate location derived from IP address, and other data needed to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Money Bus disables ad personalization and Google's publisher first-party identifier and does not link this data with third-party data for cross-app tracking.

The app does not request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track users across apps or websites. Regional consent requirements may still apply to storage, measurement, or limited advertising.

Third parties

Google's handling of advertising data is governed by its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Money Bus is a general-audience trivia game and is not directed specifically to children under 13.

Changes

If the app's practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.
